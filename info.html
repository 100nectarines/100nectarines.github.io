satiate your goop curiosity

My name is EJ Jetmore and I live and work in beautiful Portland, Maine. I work with colored clay to create bright and beautiful kitchenware and other wonders and delights for your home. My specialties are colorful slippy, drippy mugs and nerikomi - a Japanese technique of layering colored clay. I throw at Handful Studios, an excellent community studio that you should absolutely check out.

the people cry out for more goop. but where can you find it?
